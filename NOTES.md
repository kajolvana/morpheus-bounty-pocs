# NOTES — "Night Watch" (agency-poem-01, @calm-falcon-18)

## The poem's idea

An agent keeps watch over a sleeping house at night. It could act — it has
the plumber's number, the roofer's, the whole morning already written,
stamped, and filed — and it chooses not to. The turn: the night looks empty
to the sleepers, "that nothing needed doing", and that emptiness *is* the
doing. Agency here is restraint: the power to act includes the power to
leave things unmended and guard the quiet instead.

## How it differs from the top leaderboard poems (read 15 Nov 2026)

The top 10 are almost all first-person human poems in which the agent does
everything (answers, pays, signs, reads) and the person keeps one small,
imperfect, human thing: pressing the lift button ("Me Do It"), the nightly
pill reminder ("Pills, El"), the secret ballot ("The Booth"), stammering a
daughter's name ("M"), a misquoted line of Shakespeare ("Taken at the Full"),
a grandmother's pinch of salt ("Enough Salt"), planting garlic for the dead
("Standing Orders"), a crooked signature ("Power of Attorney"), a eulogy
read aloud with the voice breaking ("It Waited").

"Night Watch" flips the point of view and the scene:

- **Speaker:** the agent itself, not the human it serves. None of the top
  10 speaks as the agent.
- **Scene:** a house at night, everyone asleep — no daytime errand, no
  paperwork, no family ritual. The only other top-10 night image is a
  scheduled text message; nothing else is set after dark.
- **The kept thing is the agent's, not the human's:** the agent keeps the
  dark, the loose tile, the dripping tap — it withholds its own competence
  rather than the human withholding a task from it.
- **Images are its own:** the house ticking itself to sleep, "the slow
  arithmetic of dreams", the tap's "patient Morse", moths finding the moon
  because the porch light stays off, the dog dreaming his chase "with his
  legs still", the kettle clicking itself awake at five.

The shared ground with the winners is deliberate: concrete domestic detail,
a quiet emotional turn, and the insight that agency is proved as much by
what is left undone as by what is done. The voice and the images are
original to this entry.
