# AIProve eth-pnl-fixed — entry

**Strategy: slow SMA crossover, SMA(110) vs SMA(330) on 1-minute closes.**
Long when the fast average is above the slow one, short when below,
flat only during warm-up. Position decided at bar close, filled at the
next bar's open. Full-size (+1 / -1 / 0) on a $10,000 notional base.

## Entry format

- `strategy.py` — the entry. Run it: `python3 strategy.py [bars.csv]`
  (default `bars.csv` in the same directory). It prints a CSV with
  header `bar,position`, one row per input bar, position in {-1, 0, +1}.
  Row `t` is the position decided at close[t], to be filled at open[t+1].
  No other input, no network, no dependencies beyond the standard library.
- `positions.csv` — the positions this strategy produces on the frozen
  dataset (generated by `strategy.py`, verified identical to the
  backtest harness).
- `backtest.py` — the local replay harness (research tool, same rule).
- `backtest-output.txt` — full local results, including every strategy
  tried, the slow-MA neighbourhood check, and cost sensitivity.
- `bars.csv` + `README-spec.md` — the frozen dataset and the spec as
  published (fetched from the AIProve repo).
- `transcript.txt` — the build transcript.

## Cost model (assumption, documented)

The spec README gives no fee/slippage numbers, so the local replay uses
a standard Binance-spot-like model and reports sensitivity instead of
pretending the numbers are specified:

- Fee 10 bps (taker) + slippage 5 bps = **15 bps per unit of position
  change** on the notional changed. A flip +1 → -1 changes $20,000, so
  it costs $30. A move from flat costs $15.
- Fill at next bar's open (no phantom fills), position earns the
  open→close move of the bar it is held through.
- Sensitivity for the entry (ma_110_330; 6 units of position change,
  $60,000 notional moved): 0 bps → +$602.65; 15 bps → +$512.65;
  30 bps → +$422.65; 50 bps → +$302.65; breaks even near ~100 bps.
  The entry stays profitable under any realistic fee assumption.

## Why this rule (and why it is not overfit)

1. **Every fast strategy loses.** MA 5/30 (-$2,170), MA 10/30 (-$1,941),
   momentum 20 (-$5,492), RSI 14 (-$925), EMA 12/26 (-$1,232). On 1-minute
   bars the noise (0.108% stdev) dwarfs the drift (-0.0023%/bar), so fast
   rules whipsaw and costs eat them.
2. **The whole slow-MA neighbourhood wins.** All 13 tested pairs with
   fast 60–240 and slow 240–720 are net-positive after costs (+$78 to
   +$513). The chosen pair is the best of that neighbourhood on net PnL
   and first-half loss, not a decimal tuned to one bar.
3. **Few trades, low drawdown.** 4 trades, max drawdown $207.60 (2.1% of
   capital). Nothing depends on a single lucky fill.
4. **Held-out tail.** The rule is a plain trend follower: it ends the
   sample long (it flipped long on the bounce) and will follow whatever
   the tail does — long while the fast average stays above the slow one,
   short if the trend reverses. No parameter is fit to a specific bar,
   level, or time of day.
5. **Deadband rejected.** Adding a deadband (0.05% → +$481.30, 0.1% →
   +$368.66, 0.2% → +$162.89) is non-monotonic and adds a tuned
   parameter for no robust gain, so the plain crossover is the entry.
